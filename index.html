Meet Vivian Callegaro

With over 25 years of yoga teaching experience and a professional background in physiology, coaching psychology and professional training, Vivian founded VC High Performance Academy to bring together education, wellbeing and sustainable performance.

Her teaching integrates traditional movement practices with neuroscience-informed coaching models. Vivian works with emerging teachers, elite artists, competitive athletes, and corporate leaders across London and internationally to develop sustainable high performance without burnout.
