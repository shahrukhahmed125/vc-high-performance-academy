OUR LEARNING LABS

Evidence-Informed Practical Mastery

Our intensive labs are hands-on educational environments. We explore somatic movement, coaching psychology, and neurophysiology with academic rigour and immediate real-world utility.

Important Disciplinary Distinction: At VC High Performance Academy, Applied Positive Psychology is taught as a formal empirical psychological and coaching discipline exploring human flourishing, optimism, and cognitive reframing — not as a yoga style or aesthetic. It pairs with somatic yoga to ensure both cognitive and bodily transformation.
