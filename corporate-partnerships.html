THE BURNOUT CRISIS IN MODERN BUSINESS

The Cost of Chronic Stress Is Measurable

In demanding professional environments, high performers are routinely pushed past their physiological capacity. The results are familiar: decision fatigue, loss of top talent, impaired emotional intelligence, and costly absenteeism.

VC High Performance Academy partners with forwards-thinking London businesses, legal firms, financial institutions, and creative agencies to replace exhaustion with sustainable operational vitality. We teach executives how to harness the autonomic nervous system to stay composed, decisive, and empathetic under continuous pressure.
