ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

A Professional Academy for Sustainable Mastery

VC High Performance Academy was founded in London to provide evidence-informed education, coaching psychology, physiology and somatic disciplines for individuals and organisations committed to high performance without burnout.

OUR ORIGIN & VISION

Beyond the Traditional Yoga Studio

For decades, personal achievement and health have been treated as conflicting forces. High achievers in competitive creative arts, corporate organisations, and intensive physical disciplines have frequently accepted burnout, chronic injuries, and mental exhaustion as the unavoidable price of excellence.

VC High Performance Academy was created to disprove this assumption. We are not a recreational yoga studio or a temporary retreat centre. We are a rigorous professional education academy based in London, offering accredited training, executive coaching, and performance science to develop durable, lifelong capability.

"Supporting individuals, professionals and organisations in developing their best possible selves through evidence-informed education, coaching, wellbeing and performance development."

FOUNDER PROFILE

Vivian Callegaro

Vivian Callegaro brings over 25 years of yoga teaching experience alongside an extensive professional background in human physiology, coaching psychology, and professional educator training.

Throughout her career, Vivian has observed firsthand how both artistic performers and corporate professionals suffer when peak performance is detached from autonomic nervous system health. By integrating classical yogic alignment and breathwork with the empirical discipline of Applied Positive Psychology, she has pioneered transformative learning methodologies including NeuroEpiGeneticYoga.

OUR FOUNDATIONAL VALUES

The Principles Guiding Every Programme

Every curriculum, corporate workshop, and coaching session at VC High Performance Academy is rooted in four non-negotiable standards.

Evidence-Informed Rigour

We ground traditional yoga in contemporary biomechanics, anatomy, and validated psychological research rather than dogmatic claims.

Sustainable High Output

Excellence must be replenishable. We build self-awareness and active recovery frameworks into every teaching and coaching module.

Embodied Coaching

Intellect alone is insufficient. Real transformation happens when cognitive coaching insights are integrated into the somatic physical body.

Inclusive Empowerment

Whether training an Olympic-level figure skater or an early-stage yoga teacher, our instruction meets practitioners where they are.
