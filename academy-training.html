PROGRAMME OVERVIEW & CERTIFICATION

200 Hours of Learning and Practice

The programme welcomes aspiring yoga teachers, fitness and wellbeing professionals, performers, and people seeking personal or professional development.

180 live contact hours

Interactive teaching, supervised practice, coaching activities, teaching development and assessments.

20 independent-study hours

Structured time for revision, assignments and preparation.

Two certificates

  • 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Certification.
  • Coaching Skills Certificate.

Certification requires successful completion of the curriculum, training hours and assessments, and settlement of tuition fees.

The programme builds on the Academy's Yoga Alliance Professionals-accredited 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training. Students can progress at different speeds; graduation attendance is not required for certification.

THE ACADEMY METHOD

A Distinctive Approach to Yoga Teaching

The School Method brings traditional yoga practice together with teaching, coaching and personal development.

  • Static and isometric asanas.
  • Natural alignment without unnecessary forced correction.
  • Conscious breathing, predominantly through the nose, and pranayama.
  • Sun Salutations as the principal dynamic yoga sequence.
  • Yoganidra, Samyama, relaxation and concentration.
  • Visualisation, positive suggestions and cognitive reframing.
  • Coaching skills and Applied Positive Psychology.
  • Character strengths, confidence, creativity and authentic self-expression.

The philosophical foundations include Samkhya and Tantra, and the eight components of Ashtanga Sadhana: Mudra, Puja, Mantra, Pranayama, Kriya, Asana, Yoganidra and Samyama.

Selected experiential workshops may include dance and joyful movement as forms of physical expression, creativity and positive psychological wellbeing.

ASSESSMENT & STUDENT DEVELOPMENT

Progressive Assessment with Constructive Feedback

Theory assessment

A written assessment covering Yoga Philosophy, Sanskrit and relevant theoretical knowledge.

Anatomy & Physiology

Two written assessments:

  1. Bones and Joints.
  2. Muscular System, Energy Systems, Cardiovascular and Respiratory Systems.

Written assessment pass mark: 60%.

Practical teaching assessment

Students develop their own yoga class throughout the programme, practise teaching, observe peers and receive constructive feedback.

The final assessment considers teaching competence, safety, class structure, communication and use of the Academy Method.

Outcomes are recorded as Competent or Not Yet Competent. One feedback-supported reassessment opportunity is included where required.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Stay Connected to a Growing Teaching Community

Academy Ambassador Programme

Selected individuals may support programme promotion and student referrals, community engagement, professional networking, Academy events, collaborative activities and connections with prospective students and partner organisations.

Referral rewards may be available under separate Ambassador agreements.

Teacher-led classes & experience

The Academy aims to facilitate venue opportunities for qualified teachers to organise regular classes and develop teaching experience.

Participating teachers organise their own teaching groups, appoint a group leader, and independently manage schedules, venue bookings, registrations, communications, payments, operating costs and teaching records. The Academy facilitates opportunities and professional connections rather than daily operations.

Senior Teacher development

Experienced teachers may have opportunities to shadow established Senior Teachers, observe training delivery and receive professional mentoring.

Senior Teacher status is awarded by the relevant accreditation body, not the Academy. Eligibility depends on that body's requirements, including documented teaching hours and evidence of regular teaching experience; verifiable timetables, teaching logs and other evidence may be required.

Future Academy training opportunities

Accredited Senior Teachers may be invited to collaborate on selected modules, online and in-person workshops, supervised teaching activities and professional development events.

Collaborations are arranged separately according to experience, suitability, availability and the Academy's programme needs.
