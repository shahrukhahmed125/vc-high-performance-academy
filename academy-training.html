INTEGRATED CURRICULUM

The Seven Core Dimensions of Training

Designed for teachers who want to facilitate genuine physical safety, cognitive clarity, and emotional transformation.

1. Traditional Yoga Education

Classical philosophical foundations, Patanjali’s Yoga Sutras contextualised for modern practitioners, pranayama breath energetics, and mindful meditation practices.

2. Anatomy & Physiology

Functional skeletal variation, fascial trains, joint mechanics, autonomic nervous system regulation (vagal tone), and injury prevention protocols.

3. Teaching Methodology

Clear verbal cueing, intelligent sequencing architecture, trauma-aware space holding, postural modifications, and hands-on assisting dynamics.

4. Applied Positive Psychology

A formal psychological and coaching discipline exploring character strengths, optimism training, cognitive reframing, and self-efficacy theory.

5. Coaching Skills for Teachers

Empathetic active listening, powerful inquiry, student goal facilitation, overcoming limiting beliefs, and establishing healthy professional boundaries.

6. NeuroEpiGeneticYoga

Our signature methodology exploring somatic movement's influence on neuroplasticity, cellular stress resilience, and gene expression pathways.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About the 200-Hour YTT

Clear answers to help you determine if our modular academy pathway is the right fit.

Most conventional yoga trainings focus solely on physical poses (asana) and basic script memorisation. VC High Performance Academy integrates 25+ years of somatic teaching with Applied Positive Psychology, neuroscience-informed coaching models, and autonomic nervous system physiology. You graduate not only with confident movement instruction, but with the psychological and coaching capability to guide clients through holistic behavioural change and stress resilience.
The curriculum is specifically crafted for busy professionals. Core theoretical modules (philosophy, anatomy, coaching psychology) are conducted live online with interactive replays. Practical hands-on teaching labs in London are modular and bookable in advance across weekend intensive dates, allowing you to build your hours at an adaptable pace.
Practitioners with existing verified credentials in physiotherapy, osteopathy, personal training, sports science, registered nursing, dance instruction, or clinical bodywork are eligible for our 50% Founding Bursary (£600 total). Simply indicate your background on the free registration form for verification.
Applied Positive Psychology is not a yoga style. It is a rigorous psychological and coaching discipline that studies human flourishing, strengths development, psychological safety, and resilient goal attainment. We teach teachers how to apply these evidence-based psychological tools alongside somatic yoga movement.
Initial registration is completely free. After our admissions team reviews your submission and confirms a cohort opening, you will receive an individual secure payment link. You may pay a £50 joining fee (credited in full to your tuition) and divide the remaining balance across five monthly instalments.
